## **The Opportunity**

B2B buyers have fundamentally changed how they purchase. **61-75% prefer a rep-free experience** (Gartner, 2025). They complete **~80% of their journey** before engaging sales. They expect **on-demand, personalized, digital experiences**.

**This is unsustainable. And it's costing us deals.**
